I am a technical engineer and a Bluetooth and personal connectivity expert with deep expertise in Adaptive Frequency Hopping (AFH) and Advanced Audio Distribution Profile (A2DP). I specialize in Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) and Bluetooth Mesh architecture and application development, excelling in Bluetooth stack optimization and debugging. I have a strong command of pairing and bonding protocols, ensuring seamless device connections.

I focus on advanced topics like Bluejacking, Bluesnarfing prevention, and Ultra-Wideband (UWB) coexistence, ensuring robust and secure connectivity. I am proficient in Bluetooth core protocols, including GAP, GATT, and L2CAP, and I optimize device-to-device communication efficiency. I also integrate Bluetooth with LTE and mobile communication technologies, leveraging Carrier Aggregation (CA) and MIMO to enhance performance.

I have a solid foundation in wireless connectivity fundamentals, including modulation techniques like QAM and OFDM, signal processing, and error correction coding (ECC). I am skilled in RF module design, such as front-end modules (FEM) and antenna beamforming, which helps me optimize signal quality. By bridging Bluetooth innovation with broader wireless technologies, I drive the development of next-generation connectivity solutions.
